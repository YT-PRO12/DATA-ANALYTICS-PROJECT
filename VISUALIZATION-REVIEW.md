# Restaurant analysis visualization review

Every plot should state the question being examined, the field definitions, and the sample used.

- Label axes with units and category names.
- Distinguish frequencies from percentages; report the denominator when comparing groups.
- Document exclusions for missing ratings and missing online-order fields.
- Do not present category totals from differently filtered subsets as directly comparable.
- Use readable colors and legends; avoid relying on color alone to encode a conclusion.
- Verify that titles and narrative match the actual plotted variable.
- Re-run the notebook from a clean kernel before exporting final visuals.

Restaurant datasets may be historical, incomplete and geographically biased. Descriptive differences are not causal relationships or guarantees about current restaurant quality.

See [reproducibility checklist](ANALYSIS_REPRODUCIBILITY.md).
